What Is a Typing Speed Test?

A typing speed test measures how quickly and accurately you can type text on a keyboard. Most typing tests measure your speed in Words Per Minute (WPM).

A good typing test should consider both speed and accuracy. Simply typing very fast is not enough if there are many typing errors.

How Does WPM Work?

WPM means Words Per Minute. A standard typing calculation generally considers one word as five typed characters, including spaces.

Standard Words = Total Typed Characters ÷ 5

For example, if you type 250 characters, that represents approximately 50 standard words.

Typing Speed Test With Accuracy

Typing accuracy shows how many characters you typed correctly compared with the total characters typed.

Accuracy = Correct Characters ÷ Total Typed Characters × 100

Improving accuracy is important because a high WPM with frequent mistakes may not be useful in practical typing work.

What Is KDPH?

KDPH means Key Depressions Per Hour. It represents the number of key depressions made during one hour.

KDPH is commonly used when evaluating typing performance for certain jobs and typing examinations.

How to Improve Your Typing Speed

  • Practice typing regularly.
  • Focus on accuracy before trying to type faster.
  • Learn proper finger positioning.
  • Avoid looking at the keyboard while typing.
  • Practice with different types of English text.
  • Take timed typing tests to measure your progress.

Regular practice can help you gradually improve both typing speed and accuracy.
